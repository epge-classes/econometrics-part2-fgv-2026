Lecture Notes

  • 01 Potential Outcomes, Causal Estimands, and Identification 2.5h Potential outcomes, ATE and ATT, selection bias, treatment-effect heterogeneity, bad controls Chapter 1: Potential Outcomes, Causal Estimands, and Identification (PDF)
  • 02 Randomized Experiments and Selection on Observables 2.5h Randomization, inference and balance, regression adjustment, propensity score, IPW, AIPW, and matching Chapter 2: Randomized Experiments and Selection on Observables (PDF)
  • 03 Instrumental Variables and Local Average Treatment Effects 3.0h Instrument validity, first stage, Wald and 2SLS, principal strata and LATE, weak instruments Chapter 3: Instrumental Variables and Local Average Treatment Effects (PDF)
  • 04 Regression Discontinuity Designs 2.5h Sharp and fuzzy RD, local polynomial estimation, bandwidth choice, bias correction, manipulation diagnostics Chapter 4: Regression Discontinuity Designs (PDF)
  • 05 Difference-in-Differences: Foundations 2.0h Canonical 2×2 DiD, parallel trends, event studies, inference and serial correlation Chapter 5: Difference-in-Differences: Foundations (PDF)
  • 06 Modern Difference-in-Differences and Event Studies 3.0h Staggered adoption, TWFE limitations, Goodman–Bacon decomposition, group-time ATT, modern event studies Chapter 6: Modern Difference-in-Differences and Event Studies (PDF)
  • 07 Synthetic Control and Comparative Case Studies 2.0h Synthetic control construction, donor pool, pre-treatment fit, placebos, extensions and links to DiD Chapter 7: Synthetic Control and Comparative Case Studies (PDF)
  • 08 Diagnostics, Sensitivity Analysis, Robustness, and Research Design 2.5h Identification robustness, placebos, sensitivity analysis, cluster-robust inference, external validity, design audit Chapter 8: Diagnostics, Sensitivity Analysis, Robustness, and Research Design (PDF)

Dataset codebook: job_training.csv (Problem Set 1, Problem 10)

2,935 workers: 185 program participants (sample = program), 260 experimental controls (experimental_control), and 2,490 survey respondents (survey). Comma-separated with header row.

Variables in job_training.csv
Variable Description
id Worker identifier
sample program, experimental_control, or survey
treat 1 if the worker participated in the program; 0 otherwise
age Age in years
educ Years of schooling
black, hisp 1 if Black; 1 if Hispanic
married 1 if married
nodegree 1 if no high-school degree
re74, re75 Pre-program earnings in 1974 and 1975 (US dollars)
re78 Post-program earnings in 1978 (US dollars, outcome variable)
